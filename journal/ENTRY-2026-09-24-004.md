  at every depth (`strip_markers`); `_messages_are_prefix` and breakpoint
  counting use the same recursive predicate.
- `minicache/simulator.py`: OpenAI model matches the LONGEST COMMON PREFIX
  against stored entries (≥1,024 tokens, rounded down to 128-token blocks),
  refreshes the matched entry's last-use; Anthropic 1-hour TTL uses
  documented terms (2× write, 2,048-token minimum); summaries report raw
  read/write/uncached token categories.

  threaded end-to-end; sweep `none` = env changes AND tool churn off.

  the trace filename and churn flag.

## Conclusions that changed

- **The sweep ranking is churn-conditional.** PFS first in all 28
  churn-active conditions (both models), but under TRUE no-churn (the
  Round-2 fix) MC edges ahead by 0.8–2.4 pp in the six cells without
  mid-compaction — with nothing left to invalidate, TTL maintenance (D6,
  MC only) decides. No-churn + mid-compaction still favours PFS by
  3.4–6.3 pp: compaction is itself a cache disturbance that cache-aware
  compaction resists. No-churn/no-compaction cells sit at LCP exactly
  1.000 for every archetype (the null case). The old "37/37" claim was an
  artifact of the churn that survived the broken `none` schedule.
- **TTL maintenance is the lever that matters exactly when content
  stability is saturated** — a sharper statement than Entry 003's
  "warmer is pure composition". Turn-only, the warmer still does nothing
  in this schedule; cross-condition it is the difference-maker at zero
  churn.
- All Entry-003 ablation conclusions are unchanged in direction and
  magnitude (deltas within 0.1 pp).
