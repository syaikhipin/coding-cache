## New experiment: A6 RC+warmer, B5 MC+per_stratum_bp

Two grid cells were untested at Stage 3. Running them **changed a conclusion**:

| flip | Δ hit (all-request) | Δ hit (turn-only) |
|---|---|---|
| A6 RC+warmer   | +14.4 pp | **+0.0 pp** |
| B5 MC+per_stratum_bp | 0.0 pp | 0.0 pp |
| C5 PFS+warmer (re-run) | +5.7 pp | **+0.0 pp** |
| B2 MC−warmer (re-run)  | −7.8 pp | +1.8 pp |

**Warmer retraction.** Entry 001's reading "warmer is worth ≈8pp to MC (TTL
refresh)" conflated two axes. Warmer requests are exact re-sends of the prior
request and hit their own cache entries at 100%, so including them inflates any
all-request aggregate. On the content axis (turn-only), the warmer adds
**zero** for RC and PFS, and +1.8 pp for MC. Entry 001's D6 narrative is
retracted; the manuscript now reports turn-only hit ratios alongside
all-request ratios in Tables 2–3 and Fig 2c, and the Discussion elevates the
composition artefact to a measurement-methodology point.

## PFS 120 s mechanism (introspected from cache entries)

Compaction at step 7 resets the prefix; the only entry matching the
post-compaction turn is a shallow 569-token entry written at turn 1. At 30
s/step it survives (age 210 s < 300 s TTL) and turn 8 reads 84.8%. At 120
s/step it has expired (age > 300 s) and turn 8 reads 0% — exactly one turn
pays full price, hence the flat −3.5 pp.
