# Entry 001 — 2026-09-22 — Archetype baselines + single-dimension ablation grid

## Setup

- api_mode = `script` (deterministic scripted agent; no model nondeterminism
  confound — every archetype sees a byte-identical transcript and schedule)
- 12 steps, seed 0, compaction threshold 1500 approx-tokens (fires at the same
  step for every archetype)
- env mutations: date rollover at step 4, git-status change at step 8
- tool-set churn (re-resolved modes only): `glob` absent steps 3–5, back at 6
- tokenizer: 4 chars ≈ 1 token, identical across archetypes; between-archetype
  deltas are the quantity of interest
- archetypes (design dimensions D1–D6, see README):
  **PFS** prefix-frozen stratified / **RC** recomputed-context /
  **MC** minimal-context
- simulator corrections applied before these runs (recorded for provenance):
  1. cache entries written at EVERY breakpoint, not only the deepest
     (≤4 per request, per Anthropic documented semantics)
  2. `cache_control` markers stripped from canonical token streams
     (transport metadata, not cached content)
  3. same stripping applied to region mapping
  4. scripted tool results embed real fixture content; step 0 reads README,
     so the transcript reaches the compaction threshold

## E1 — wire-level prefix stability (turn requests)

| archetype | LCP ratio | invalidation events | breakpoints |
|---|---|---|---|
| PFS | 0.897 | 0 | 4 |
| RC  | 0.517 | 4 (steps 3,6 tools; 4,8 system) | 3 |
| MC  | 0.632 | 3 (steps 3,6 tools; 8 system) | 3 |

PFS is the only archetype with zero invalidations; its residual LCP loss is
the trailing-growth term (append), not prefix breakage. RC/MC invalidations
land exactly on the scheduled env changes (steps 4, 8 → system region) and
tool-churn window edges (3, 6 → tools region), confirming the causal path
churn-source → prompt region → invalidation.

## E3 — single-dimension ablations (delta vs the archetype's own baseline)

| flip | Δ LCP | Δ Anthropic hit | reading |
|---|---|---|---|
| RC+frozen env (D3)         | +0.126 | **+24.8pp** | env churn is RC's largest single loss |
| RC+frozen tools (D5)       | +0.243 | **+22.1pp** | tool churn comparable to env churn |
| RC+stratified3 (D1)        | +0.024 | +4.7pp  | structure helps mildly alone |

| MC−warmer (D6)             | 0.000  | **−7.8pp** | warmer is worth ≈8pp to MC (TTL refresh) |
| MC+frozen env (D3)         | +0.012 | +2.7pp  | MC's env block has no date → git-only churn |

| PFS−frozen tools (D5)      | −0.234 | **−11.2pp** | removing D5 likewise |
| PFS−cache_aware comp. (D4) | −0.008 | −0.3pp  | D4 near-neutral once per compaction event |
| PFS−stratified3 (D1)       | −0.003 | −0.5pp  | D1 near-neutral in isolation |
| PFS+warmer (D6)            | 0.000  | +5.7pp  | warmer helps even PFS under this timing model |

**Finding (interaction structure).** The five design dimensions decompose
into two classes with qualitatively different effects:

- **Stability dimensions** (D3 env freshness, D5 tool stability) dominate:
  each worth 10–25pp of hit ratio, symmetric across directions (adding them
  to RC/MC and removing them from PFS moves the metric by comparable
  magnitudes).
- **Placement dimensions** (D1 stratification, D2 breakpoints) and **policy
  dimensions** (D4 compaction) are conditional: near-neutral in isolation,
  effective only on top of stability. Prefix stability is the binding
  constraint; placement and compaction policy are second-order refinements.
- **TTL maintenance** (D6 warmer) is orthogonal to both: it acts on the time
  axis, not the content axis.

## Sensitivity (TTL × step timing)

At 120 s/step with 300 s TTL, MC loses ~9pp (70.4%→61.0%): TTL expiry bites
exactly when turns are slow, and the warmer (D6) partially compensates.
PFS loses ~3pp under the same stress because its deep breakpoints refresh
TTL as a side effect of normal traffic. RC is flat (nothing left to expire).

## Threats to validity / notes

- Tokenizer approximation (4 chars/token) is shared across archetypes;
  absolute values are approximate, deltas are the measurement.
- Churn rates (env changes at steps 4/8, tool churn at 3–6) are scheduled and
  identical across archetypes; they model churn observed in the audit but the
  rate is a modeling choice — sweepable in future entries.
- The simulator implements documented provider semantics, not the providers;
  the study's claims are about the method comparison under those semantics.
